Your website looks fine. Is it helping your business grow?

Most businesses don't have a design problem. They have a gap between the people who visit and the customers who buy. We recognise these signs:

  • An outdated site that doesn't reflect the quality of your business
  • A poor mobile experience, where most visitors arrive
  • Visitors who browse and leave without becoming leads
  • Enquiries copied by hand from one place to another
  • Leads that go cold because follow-up is slow or forgotten
  • Inconsistent social media posting
  • Tools that don't talk to each other
  • No clear view of what's working

Joey Technology connects the pieces, so your website, your tools, and your follow-up work as one system.

How the system works

We build systems, not isolated websites.

Every project is designed around the path a customer takes, from first visit to repeat business.

  1. Attract

    Website, social media, and search bring the right people in.

  2. Capture

    Forms, WhatsApp, and lead magnets collect their details.

  3. Qualify

    Automation and AI sort serious enquiries from casual ones.

  4. Follow up

    Email, WhatsApp, and CRM keep every lead warm.

  5. Convert

    Sales, bookings, and purchases happen with less friction.

  6. Grow

    Analytics show what to improve and what to automate next.

For example: instead of copying leads from your website into a spreadsheet by hand, a workflow captures the lead, organises the details, notifies your team, and starts the follow-up.

FAQ

Questions business owners ask us

How much does a website cost?

It depends on scope: the number of pages, the features, and whether automation is included. After a short conversation we send a clear estimate, so you know what you're paying for before work starts.

How long does a website project take?

A focused business website typically takes a few weeks. Projects with automation, integrations, or custom features take longer. We give you a timeline once we understand the scope.

Do you work with businesses outside Nigeria?

Yes. We work with clients in Nigeria and internationally, and communicate by video call, email, and WhatsApp.

Do you provide website maintenance?

Yes. Maintenance and growth plans cover updates, security, backups, performance checks, and improvements.

Can you automate our existing processes?

In most cases, yes. We start by mapping how the process works today, then automate the steps that are repetitive and keep a person involved where judgement matters.

Can you connect WhatsApp, email, CRM, or payment systems?

We integrate the tools you already use wherever they offer an API or a supported connection. We confirm what's possible for your specific setup during discovery.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We review what's working, keep what should be kept, and rebuild what isn't earning its place.

Do you provide hosting?

We recommend and set up reliable hosting for your project, and you keep ownership of your accounts and content.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes. After launch, we offer handover support and optional monthly plans for maintenance, improvement, and monitoring of your automations.
