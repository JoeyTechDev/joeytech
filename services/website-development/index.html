FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a business website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, the features and whether automation is included. We scope your project first and send a clear estimate, so you know what you are paying for before work begins.

How long does a website take to build?

A focused business website usually takes a few weeks. Projects with bookings, portals or integrations take longer, and we confirm the timeline once the scope is clear.

Should I choose WordPress or a custom website?

WordPress suits most business sites that need easy editing. A custom build suits complex logic, portals and dashboards. We recommend whichever fits your goals.

Do you build websites for businesses outside Nigeria?

Yes. We work with clients in Nigeria and internationally, and communicate by video call, email and WhatsApp.
