Email marketing automation that follows up for you

How it works

Our process, step by step

  1. Plan

    We define your audiences and what each should hear and when.

  2. Write and build

    We set up the sequences, triggers and templates.

  3. Connect

    We link your website, forms and CRM so contacts enter the right sequence.

  4. Improve

    We review the numbers and refine the messages.

Technology we use: Email platforms such as Mailchimp or Brevo, n8n, your website forms and CRM, and domain email authentication.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which email tool should I use?

It depends on your list size, budget and the features you need. We recommend a tool after understanding your goals and connect it to your website and CRM.

Can emails be triggered by what people do?

Yes. Sign-ups, form submissions, bookings and purchases can each start a different sequence.

Will my emails reach the inbox?

No one can guarantee inbox placement, but correct domain authentication and good list practices make it far more likely.

Do you write the emails?

We can draft the sequences with you, based on your offer and tone, and you approve them before they go live.
