Website Maintenance and Growth

Website maintenance and growth plans that keep your site fast, secure and improving

A website is not finished at launch. Software ages, security threats appear and visitor needs change. Joey Technology offers maintenance and growth plans so your site stays reliable, and keeps getting better at turning visitors into customers.

How it works

Our process, step by step

  1. Review

    We audit your site's health, speed, security and tracking.

  2. Plan

    We agree what the monthly plan covers.

  3. Maintain

    We run updates, backups and checks on a regular schedule.

  4. Report and improve

    You receive a short report and recommendations.

Technology we use: WordPress, PHP, MySQL, caching and security tools, uptime monitoring, Google Analytics and Search Console.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does website maintenance include?

Updates, backups, security checks, performance monitoring, and small content or layout changes. Your plan lists exactly what is covered.

Do you maintain sites you did not build?

Usually yes. We start with a review of the site to understand its condition before agreeing a plan.

What happens if my site goes down?

We monitor availability, investigate quickly and restore from a backup if needed.

Can I cancel a maintenance plan?

Plans are monthly, and we confirm the terms with you before you start.
