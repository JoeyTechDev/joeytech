FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When do I need a custom application instead of WordPress?

When your process involves logins, roles, complex rules, custom dashboards or data that must be stored and queried, a custom application is usually the better fit.

Which technologies do you use?

Front end with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React; backends in PHP, Node.js or Python; and databases such as MySQL, PostgreSQL or MongoDB. We choose based on your needs.

Can you extend a system I already have?

Often yes. We review the existing code and data first, then agree what can be improved or added.

Can I see examples?

Yes. Our work page includes interactive demos for a school website with portals, a hotel site, and a courier tracking platform.
