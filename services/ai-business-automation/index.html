FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What can be automated in my business?

Lead capture, follow-up emails and messages, appointment reminders, reporting, invoice reminders, onboarding steps and data syncing between tools are common starting points.

Do I have to change the tools I already use?

Usually not. We connect the tools you already use, wherever they offer an API or a supported connection, and confirm what is possible during discovery.

Is it safe to use AI with customer data?

We limit what data is sent to AI services, choose providers carefully, and keep a person in the loop for anything sensitive or customer-facing.

How long does an automation project take?

A single workflow can be ready within days. Larger systems with several integrations take longer, and we agree the timeline before we begin.
