Lead Generation Systems

Lead generation systems that capture, qualify and follow up automatically

Getting traffic is only half the job. A lead generation system makes sure every visitor who shows interest is captured, organised and followed up quickly. Joey Technology connects your website, forms, WhatsApp, CRM and email into one reliable path from enquiry to conversation.

How it works

Our process, step by step

  1. Audit

    We review how leads reach you today and where they are lost.

  2. Design the path

    We plan the capture points, the qualifying questions and the follow-up.

  3. Build and connect

    We build pages and workflows and connect them to your tools.

  4. Refine

    We review results and improve the path over time.

Technology we use: WordPress or custom pages, Netlify Forms or Web3Forms, n8n, Google Sheets or a CRM, and email and WhatsApp integrations.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a lead generation system?

It is the combination of pages, forms, tools and automations that capture an interested visitor and move them toward a conversation, without manual effort at each step.

Do I need a CRM?

Not at the start. A well-organised spreadsheet can work well. We recommend a CRM when volume or team size makes one worthwhile.

Can leads come through WhatsApp?

Yes. We can add WhatsApp entry points to your website and route those conversations into the same pipeline.

Will this work for a small business?

Yes. We start with a simple system and add steps as your volume grows.
