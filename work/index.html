Evergreen Academy: school website and portal demo

A school website that brings admissions, academics and parent and student portal access together.

View live demo (opens in a new tab)
Read case study
ChallengeParents and students need to find admissions information, news and their portals quickly, and schools need a professional public face that connects to their internal systems.

SolutionA responsive school website with an admissions call to action, academics and student life sections, parent and student portal entry points, and a news and events feed.

CourierLab: courier tracking and quote demo

Shipment tracking, instant delivery pricing and pickup booking for a courier company.

View live demo (opens in a new tab)
Read case study
ChallengeCustomers want to know where their parcel is and what a delivery will cost, without calling the office.

SolutionA tracking page with shipment status and route history, an instant price calculator, service options and a pickup booking call to action.

TechnologyHTML, CSS and JavaScript front end with sample data, hosted on Netlify. A production version would add a backend API and database.

Intended outcomeDesigned to reduce "where is my parcel?" calls and make it easy to get a quote and book a pickup.

Business automation with n8n

Workflows that capture leads, notify teams and trigger follow-up across the tools a business already uses.

Read case study
ChallengeEnquiries arrived from several places and were handled by hand, so follow-up was slow and details were easy to lose.

SolutionAn automated workflow that captures each enquiry, stores it in a CRM or sheet, alerts the team and starts an email or WhatsApp follow-up, with AI used to sort requests where it helps.

Technologyn8n, webhooks, APIs, email and messaging integrations, and AI models.

Intended outcomeDesigned to cut manual data handling and make sure every enquiry gets a prompt, consistent response.
