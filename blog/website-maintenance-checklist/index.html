Website Maintenance Checklist: What to Do Weekly, Monthly and Yearly

By Joey Technology · 9 November 2026 · 3 min read

Websites need regular care. Software updates, security patches, expiring certificates and broken forms are all easy to miss, and each can cost you customers. Use this schedule to keep your site healthy.

Weekly

  • Check that the site loads and key pages work.
  • Submit a test enquiry to confirm forms and notifications arrive.
  • Review any security alerts and spam comments.
  • Confirm that backups completed.

Monthly

  • Update WordPress core, themes and plugins, after taking a backup.
  • Remove unused plugins and users.
  • Test your site speed on mobile.
  • Review Google Search Console for errors and Analytics for trends.
  • Check for broken links.
  • Test that a backup can actually be restored.

Quarterly

  • Review page content for accuracy, prices and contact details.
  • Update your most important pages based on what visitors do.
  • Review your automations and integrations.

Yearly

  • Renew your domain, and confirm that hosting and SSL are current.
  • Review design and messaging against your business goals.
  • Audit user accounts, passwords and access.
  • Review your hosting plan and performance.

Why this matters

Outdated software is a common route for attacks, slow pages lose visitors, and a broken contact form silently loses leads. Regular care protects your reputation and search visibility.
