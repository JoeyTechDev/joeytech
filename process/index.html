Process

A clear process, so you always know what happens next.

Every project follows the same seven stages. You'll see progress at each one, and nothing goes live without your approval.

  1. Discover

    We learn about your business, your audience, your goals, and the problems you want solved.

  2. Strategise

    We define the website or system structure and the path that turns visitors into enquiries.

  3. Design

    We create the user experience and visual direction, and you review it before we build.

  4. Build

    We develop the project and connect the forms, tools, and integrations it needs.

  5. Test

    We check performance, mobile behaviour, forms, integrations, security, and usability.

  6. Launch

    We deploy the project, hand it over, and support you through the first days live.

  7. Grow

    We keep improving through optimisation, maintenance, new automation, and regular reporting.
